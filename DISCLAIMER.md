## Limitations and Risks

### Activation Maximization Limitations
- **Instability**: Generated patterns may be unstable and vary significantly across runs
- **Misleading Interpretations**: Visualizations may not accurately represent actual model behavior
- **Local Optima**: Optimization may converge to local minima, producing suboptimal results
- **Computational Variability**: Results may depend on random initialization and computational precision

### Interpretability Concerns
- **No Ground Truth**: There is no definitive "correct" interpretation of neural network behavior
- **Method Dependencies**: Results depend heavily on hyperparameters and regularization choices
- **Context Sensitivity**: Interpretations may not generalize across different datasets or models
- **Human Bias**: Visualizations may be subject to human interpretation biases

### Technical Limitations
- **Computational Requirements**: Activation maximization requires significant computational resources
- **Hyperparameter Sensitivity**: Results are highly sensitive to optimization parameters
- **Model Architecture Dependencies**: Effectiveness varies across different network architectures
- **Dataset Dependencies**: Results may not generalize across different datasets
